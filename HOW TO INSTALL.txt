SHOT LIST - standalone app (no Claude account needed)

The app is the 6 files in this folder. It must be served from an https website
for the iPhone "Add to Home Screen" and offline mode to work. Free option:

GitHub Pages (free, permanent)
 1. Make a free account at github.com (on your Mac).
 2. Click "+" (top right) > New repository. Name it e.g. shot-list. Set Public. Create.
 3. On the new repository page click "uploading an existing file".
    Drag in all 6 files from this folder (index.html, manifest.json, sw.js,
    icon-180.png, icon-192.png, icon-512.png). Click "Commit changes".
 4. Settings > Pages > Source: "Deploy from a branch", Branch: main, folder / (root). Save.
 5. Wait about a minute. Your app address is:
    https://<your-github-username>.github.io/shot-list/

On your iPhone
 1. Open that address in Safari.
 2. Tap Share > Add to Home Screen > Add.
 3. Always open it from the Home Screen icon - the Home Screen app keeps its
    own saved data, separate from the Safari tab.

Your data
 - Lists and ticks are saved on the phone only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
 - Back up from the ••• button > Backup & restore (save a file or copy the text
   into Notes). Restore the same way on a new phone.
 - Deleting the Home Screen icon deletes its data, so back up first.

Updating the app later
 Upload the new index.html AND sw.js to the same repository (replace the old ones):
 repository page > "Add file" > "Upload files" > drag both in > "Commit changes".
 Check it went live: open https://<your-github-username>.github.io/shot-list/ in Safari -
 the version next to "SHOT LIST" at the top should match the new one. (This can take 1-2 minutes.)
 Then on the phone: open the Home Screen app, wait 5 seconds, swipe it closed, open it again.
 From version v12.01 on, later updates show on the first open.
 Your lists and ticks stay on the phone. A new batch list is added automatically
 and opens first; a list you deleted is never added back.

 v12.01 (28 Nov): adds "Photo list 2026.12.01" (Batch 24, 25 lines, 66 photos) and makes
 the app always load the newest version from GitHub when online.

If the list still doesn't appear
 Use the list picker at the top (tap the list name) - "Photo list 2026.12.01" may be there.
 Or tap "+ New" and paste the Category / Subcategory / Up To table from the assignment email.
